Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Rungs is a word ladder game. This page explains what information the app collects and why.

What's stored only on your device

Your puzzle progress, streak, hint usage, and difficulty preference are saved locally on your device (browser or app storage) so your game picks up where you left off. This data never leaves your device.

What's sent to our server

When you solve the daily puzzle, the app sends the following to our server to run the daily leaderboard:

  • A randomly generated anonymous ID (not linked to your name, email, or any account — it's just a random identifier stored on your device)
  • A display name you choose to show on the leaderboard (or "Anonymous" if you skip this)
  • Your solve time, number of steps, and number of hints used for that day's puzzle

We don't require or collect your real name, email address, or any other personally identifying information to play the game or appear on the leaderboard.

How this data is used

Solely to display the daily leaderboard (fastest solvers and first-to-solve) to other players. We don't use it for advertising profiles, and we don't sell it to anyone.

Third parties

The app is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes requests and stores leaderboard data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider. The app does not currently include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Data retention

Leaderboard entries are kept indefinitely as a historical record of daily results. You can clear your locally stored data at any time by clearing the app's storage/data or uninstalling it.

Children's privacy

Rungs is a general-audience word game and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
