FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars):
Rungs is a daily word ladder puzzle: turn a start word into a goal word by changing one letter at a time, with every step along the way a real word.

DAILY CHALLENGE
Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. Solve it, then compare your steps and hints against the leaderboard to see if you were first to solve it or the fastest.

PRACTICE MODE
Not feeling competitive? Practice mode gives you unlimited puzzles across three difficulty levels — Easy, Medium, and Hard — based on word length, so you can warm up or challenge yourself any time.

STUCK? WATCH AN AD FOR A HINT
Get a nudge in the right direction: watching a short ad highlights which letter to change next. It won't hand you the answer, just point you at where to look. (Using a hint on the daily puzzle keeps you off the ranked leaderboard, but you'll still show up as "assisted.")

LEADERBOARD
Compete for first-to-solve and fastest-time bragging rights on the daily puzzle under your own anonymous name — no account required.

Turn the ladder, one rung at a time.
